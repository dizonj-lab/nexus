AI and ML models

    
NEXUS starts with a small language model for entity enrichment and advice, plus an ML model for load forecasting. An LLM can be switched on for deeper reasoning. Every model call is written to the AI log with its explanation.

    
    
    
    
In this prototype the advisor runs as a deterministic rules engine standing in for the SLM. Explanations in the AI log show exactly which rule fired and why.
